# Polymateria

> Polymateria Ltd is a British materials-science company based at the Imperial College I-HUB,
> White City Campus, London. It develops Biotransformation, a masterbatch additive blended into
> polyethylene and polypropylene at roughly 2% by weight so that plastic escaping a waste stream
> weathers into a bioavailable wax and fully biodegrades in the open environment without leaving
> microplastics. Polymateria co-authored BSI PAS 9017, the first standard for measuring the
> biodegradability of polyolefins.

## What an agent should know first

Polymateria sells a chemical additive, not software. As of 2026-08-26 it operates **no public API,
no developer portal, no SDK, and no machine-readable API contract**. Contract discovery was run
against every host the company controls (www.polymateria.com plus the api/docs/developer/app/portal
subdomains, all of which are NXDOMAIN) and found no OpenAPI, GraphQL, MCP or A2A surface. There is
nothing here for an agent to call. Commercial contact is a human one: info@polymateria.com.

The one machine-readable surface on the domain is the stock WordPress REST API at
https://www.polymateria.com/wp-json/ — the CMS behind the marketing site, not a Polymateria
product. Do not treat it as a company API.
